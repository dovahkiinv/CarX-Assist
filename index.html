🎯 Master Tuning Reference (0% vs 100% Impact Guide)

Understand exactly how every single slider and assist checkbox mathematically alters car dynamics in Assetto Corsa. Compare the physical extremes to dial in your personal drift style.

Steering Geometry

Max Drift Lock Angle (35° – 75°)

Defines the physical front steering lock limit. Crucial to match your drift pack's steering rack geometry.

At 35.0° (Street OEM Lock):
Tight factory steering angle. Suitable for subtle street slides, but will instantly spin on deep drift angles.
At 75.0° (Extreme Pro Angle):
Enormous steering travel allowing insane backwards entries and deep slip angles without spinning out.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: Street cars (WDT Street) use 48°. Pro cars (WDT Pro, Gravy) use 53°–55°. Formula Drift / VDC uses 60°–68°. Match this value to your car pack!
Drift Line Lock

Anti-Straighten Lock (Inward Keep)

Limits how far wheels can turn inward toward the turn apex during active drift. Prevents the car from suddenly gripping up and violently snapping straight.

At 0% (Full Manual Angle Control):
No inward steering angle clamping. The driver can steer all the way inward to straighten the car instantly. Requires high stick precision.
At 85% (Maximum Lockout):
Heavily prevents wheels from turning inside the turn while sliding. The car stays locked in drift angle even if you push the stick inwards.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 40%–50% is the golden sweet spot for authentic CarX feel: forgiving mid-drift stability without feeling glued or artificial.
Core Assist Autocatch

Idle Countersteer Ratio

Determines the automatic countersteer angle applied when the thumbstick is released to center (idle).

At 50% (Weak Autocatch):
Wheels countersteer only half of the required angle when centering the stick. The car will spin if you do not actively push the stick into counter.
At 100% (Full Mathematical Autocatch):
Releasing the stick perfectly locks wheels at the kinematic countersteer angle. Makes flick entries and high-speed chicanes effortless.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: Set to 80% for authentic CarX street feel, or 90%–100% if you are learning or prefer effortless tandem transitions.
Gyro Stabilization

Drift Damping (StabK)

Calculates angular velocity damping to eliminate high-frequency steering wobble, oscillation, and tank-slappers.

At 0.00 (Unfiltered Raw):
Zero damping on angular velocity. The car reacts with lightning speed but may wobble or fish-tail wildly on high grip tires.
At 0.35 (Heavy Damping):
Maximum stabilizing resistance. Completely eliminates tank-slappers and wobble, but transitions will feel slower and more deliberate.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 0.10–0.14 gives flawless high-speed stability on 99% of Assetto Corsa drift tracks without muting car agility.
Rack Speed

Steering Speed (SmoothK1)

The base rotational speed of the virtual steering rack in radians per second during transitions.

At 1.5 rad/s (Slow & Heavy):
Very slow steering rack. Suitable for heavy GT or highway cruisers, but will lag behind fast drift transitions.
At 14.0 rad/s (Instant Twitch):
Near instantaneous wheel repositioning. Wheels snap from lock to lock immediately. Recommended for competition pro tandem drifters.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 5.0–6.0 rad/s simulates realistic wheel mass for street cars; 7.5–9.0 rad/s provides razor-sharp pro drift transitions.
Dynamic Snap Boost

Yaw Counter Boost (SmoothK2)

Multiplies countersteer speed when the car rotates aggressively (high angular velocity), catching violent snap oversteer.

At 0.00 (Fixed Steering Speed):
Steering rack moves at constant speed regardless of violent snap oversteer. Requires early manual anticipation.
At 4.00 (Instant Snap Saver):
Instantly flings front wheels into counter at four times base speed during aggressive inertia kicks, catching almost any entry.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 1.50–2.00 provides the ideal balance between realistic physical feedback and catching sudden aggressive entries.
Latency Killer

Prediction 0.2s Lookahead Gain

Anticipates vehicle trajectory 200 milliseconds into the future based on lateral Gs and yaw rate to eliminate gamepad input lag.

At 0.00 (Reactive Only):
Assist calculates counter purely based on current angles. Can feel slightly lagging on fast, twitchy modern drift cars.
At 2.00 (Maximum Predictive Bias):
Aggressive lookahead. Wheels start countersteering milliseconds before your eyes can even register the initiation.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 1.00 is mathematically tuned for standard 60-144Hz displays and gamepad thumbstick travel.
Throttle Modulation

Gas Trigger Curve (RT Gamma)

Shapes the throttle curve on the right trigger (RT). 1.00 is linear; higher values soften initial trigger travel for high-horsepower cars.

At 0.50 (Aggressive Bite):
Huge power delivered right at the top of the trigger. Great for low-powered naturally aspirated cars (e.g. AE86).
At 2.50 (Ultra-Delicate Feathering):
First 70% of trigger travel delivers gentle power. Crucial for modulating 1000hp turbo monsters without spinning tires uncontrollably.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 1.00–1.20 provides the most natural throttle feathering in high-angle drift zones.
Left-Foot Braking

Brake Sensitivity (LT Gamma)

Shapes the left trigger (LT) brake progression for delicate left-foot braking and mid-drift clipping point adjustments.

At 0.50 (Hard Bite):
Full braking pressure is applied with light trigger squeeze. Prone to upsetting car balance mid-drift.
At 2.50 (Gradual Trail Braking):
Extremely gradual brake buildup. Allows resting your finger on LT to adjust angle without locking wheels.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: 1.30 allows you to drag the front brakes mid-drift to tuck the nose into clipping points effortlessly.
Smart Brake Bias

Drift Brake Anti-Lock Assist (Left-Foot Braking)

Dynamically optimizes brake bias during active drift so pressing the brake pedal pitches the car without killing rear wheelspin.

Disabled [ ] (Standard AC Brake Physics):
Pressing LT mid-drift clamps all 4 wheels equally, often locking the rear differential and killing drift momentum.
Enabled [✔] (Smart Drift Bias Optimizer):
Prioritizes front brake bite to transfer weight forward and tighten drift line, leaving rear tires spinning freely.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: Keep [✔] ON at all times for pro-level left-foot braking techniques on gamepad.
Freestyle & Gymkhana

Reverse Entry & 360 Spin Recovery (>90° / >140°)

Special physics algorithm that inverts steering lock past 90° so you can initiate backward entries and complete 360° spins.

Disabled [ ] (90° Strict Clamp):
Countersteer stops at 90°. Going past 90° will force maximum countersteer and cause an instant spinout.
Enabled [✔] (Freestyle Angle Inversion):
Inverts steering logic through 90°–180°, guiding wheels smoothly through full 360° rotations back into forward drift.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: Enable [✔] if you do gymkhana, freestyle, or backwards entries into high-speed banked corners!
Transmission Control

CarX Drift Auto-Gearbox Mode

Drift-optimized automatic gearbox logic that locks gears in peak torque band and eliminates mid-drift shifting.

Disabled [ ] (Standard AC Automatic):
Stock game transmission often downshifts or upshifts awkwardly mid-corner when wheelspin spikes, upsetting balance.
Enabled [✔] (CarX Style RPM Governor):
Maintains current gear throughout the entire slide, keeping the engine singing at peak torque until you straighten.
💡 Pro Tip & Sweet Spot: A must-have if you drive with automatic shifting! If you shift manually with bumpers, leave it off.

🏁 CarX Players' Transition Guide to Assetto Corsa

Why is Assetto Corsa Impossible on Gamepad Without This Script?

In vanilla Assetto Corsa, your analog stick maps directly to steering rack rotation. Unlike CarX Drift Racing Online, which features a sophisticated built-in countersteer solver, AC requires you to manually spin the wheels 900 degrees in milliseconds — a physical impossibility on a tiny 15mm thumbstick. The CarX Assist script ports the exact mathematical algorithm from CarX SteerVersion 6 directly into Custom Shaders Patch, giving you the natural steering flow of CarX combined with Assetto Corsa's unmatched physics engine.

Core Drifting Rules with CarX Assist:

  • The stick indicates your INTENTION, not raw wheel angle: To increase drift angle, pull the stick slightly in the direction of the turn. To straighten up, push toward the outside.
  • Let the assist catch the car (Idle Countersteer): When initiating a high-speed flick or transition, release the analog stick to center for a fraction of a second. The assist will catch the momentum at the optimal tire slip angle without spinning!
  • Throttle controls your trajectory line: More throttle pushes the car wider towards the outside of the track; lifting off pulls the car tighter into the inside apex.
  • Left-foot brake for speed adjustments: Keep Drift Brake Anti-Lock assist active so you can tap LT to scrub speed in tandem without washing out the front tires.
